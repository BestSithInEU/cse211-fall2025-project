\section{Problem Statement}

Design and implement a multi-drone delivery route planner over a city graph with battery and charging station constraints. The city is modeled as a weighted graph where nodes represent delivery locations, warehouses, and charging stations, and edges represent flight paths with associated energy costs. Your system must plan routes for a fleet of drones to complete all deliveries while managing battery levels and charging schedules. The planner should handle dynamic scenarios such as new delivery orders and drone failures.

\section{Core Concepts}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Battery State:} Each drone maintains a current charge level, a consumption rate per unit distance, and a maximum battery capacity. Routes must ensure charge never drops below zero.
  \item \textbf{Charging Station:} Special nodes in the graph where drones can recharge. Charging time is proportional to the amount of charge needed.
  \item \textbf{Delivery Window:} Each delivery may have time constraints specifying the earliest and latest acceptable delivery times.
  \item \textbf{Fleet Coordination:} Multiple drones operate simultaneously and may compete for shared resources such as charging stations.
  \item \textbf{Energy Cost Model:} The energy required to traverse an edge varies based on payload weight, wind conditions, and elevation changes.
\end{itemize}

\section{Key Challenges}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Feasibility Checking:} Verify that a drone can complete a proposed route without running out of battery, accounting for detours to charging stations.
  \item \textbf{Strategic Charging:} Decide when and where to charge to minimize total delivery time while avoiding battery depletion.
  \item \textbf{Route Construction:} Solve a TSP variant with battery constraints, constructing efficient routes that visit all assigned delivery points.
  \item \textbf{Dynamic Replanning:} Adapt routes in response to new delivery orders, drone failures, or changing conditions.
  \item \textbf{Conflict Resolution:} Handle scenarios where multiple drones need the same charging station simultaneously.
\end{itemize}
